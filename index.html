PhD candidate · Computer Science Education

Hi, I'm Christina.

I study how we can better teach and assess computer systems.

I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Max Fowler.

My research focuses on foundational systems knowledge, curriculum design, and assessment — particularly as generative AI changes how students learn and work with code.

I care deeply about teaching and hope to pursue a faculty career centered on education and research. I will be on the 2027-2028 academic job market!

What I'm exploring

Research

My work brings a computer systems background to questions about what students should learn, how we teach it, and how we know they understand it in the age of generative AI.

01

Systems curricula

Understanding which foundational systems topics are taught across institutions and how curricula align with disciplinary expectations

02

Assessment & reasoning

Designing assessments that reveal how students reason about code and system behavior, beyond whether they arrive at a correct answer

03

Generative AI & learning

Exploring how AI tools affect programming practices, motivation, and what it means for students to develop computing competence
